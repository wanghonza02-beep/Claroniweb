3. What Claroni does — and does not do

Claroni is a finance tracking tool. It connects to your card or account, sorts your transactions automatically, and shows you one understandable number instead of a spreadsheet nobody reads.

Claroni is not a financial adviser, a bank, or a payment institution. Nothing the app shows you is investment, tax, or legal advice. Decisions about your money are yours to make, on your own judgement or with a professional.

The figures in the app come from data your bank or card provider gives us. We work to keep them accurate, but we cannot guarantee they are always complete or up to date. For anything binding — a tax return, a loan application — always rely on the statement from your bank.

Your password is your responsibility. Do not share it, and do not reuse one from somewhere else. If you suspect someone else has reached your account, tell us as soon as you can.

Your account is personal to you. You may not share or sell it to anyone else.

5. Plans, prices and billing

Claroni offers a free Starter plan and a paid Claroni Plus plan. What each includes is set out on the pricing page. The free plan is free indefinitely and does not require a card.

The paid plan costs USD 9.99 per month. The price includes VAT where it applies. Payment is taken in advance for each billing period.

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe — we never see or store your full card number. All we receive from Stripe is confirmation that a payment succeeded, and the last four digits.

We may change our prices in future. We will tell you by email at least 30 days beforehand, and the new price will only apply from your next billing period. If you do not want to pay it, you can cancel before then at no penalty.

6. Automatic renewal and cancellation

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel it. This is so the service does not stop working on you overnight.

You can cancel at any time from Manage billing, which you will find on your dashboard and in your account menu. You do not need to write to us or call, and you do not need to give a reason.

When you cancel, your paid features stay active until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that your account returns to the free plan. We do not delete your data simply because you stopped paying.

As a consumer you have a statutory right to withdraw from this agreement within 14 days of entering into it, without giving a reason. Simply write to us at the contact address in section 16.

We refund by the same route the money arrived, within 14 days of your withdrawal at the latest.

Outside that statutory period we do not usually refund unused parts of a billing period — but if something has gone wrong on our side, get in touch. We handle those case by case and do not try to wriggle out of them.
