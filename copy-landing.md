# Claroni — Landing Page Copy (EN / CZ)

Draft v1. Written against the voice rules in Section 10 of the design system:
short sentences, one next step per section, smart friend not compliance department,
no jargon, no fear framing, no exclamation points.

Czech uses informal *ty* throughout — the brand is a friend, not an institution.

## 2. How it works — three steps

**Section heading**
EN: Three steps, then nothing.
CZ: Tři kroky a pak už nic.

**Step 1**
EN: **Connect your card** — One time, about a minute. Read-only access, nothing else.
CZ: **Propoj kartu** — Jednou, zabere to minutu. Přístup jen pro čtení, nic víc.

**Step 2**
EN: **We sort it out** — Every transaction categorized automatically. You don't tag anything.
CZ: **Zbytek uděláme my** — Každá transakce se zařadí sama. Nic neoznačuješ.

**Step 3**
EN: **You get a heads up** — When something's off, Claroni tells you before you spend. Not after.
CZ: **Ozveme se včas** — Když něco nesedí, Claroni to řekne, než utratíš. Ne až potom.

## Notes for review

1. **Czech tone** — I used informal *ty* and contractions-of-speech rhythm to match
   the "honest best friend" personality. If Claroni's Czech market skews more formal,
   the *vy* rewrite is quick but changes every section.
2. **"Waitlist"** — kept as the English loanword, which is standard in Czech startup
   copy. *Pořadník* is the native alternative but reads bureaucratic, which is the
   opposite of the brand.
3. **Section 3 vs 4** — both are "we're not like other apps" arguments. If the page
   feels repetitive when built, section 4 is the one to cut.
