About Us

The company does not exist. Claroni is fictional and this site is a practice build. Nobody operates it, there is no team behind it, and nothing described below is actually happening. The text is written the way a real company would write it.

Claroni came out of one specific frustration: finance apps are built for people with a salary. For a student, or for someone in the first months of a company, they are close to useless.

Why it exists

When money arrives irregularly, a conventional budget does not work. You cannot divide up a month you do not yet know the shape of. The app still offers you a pie chart, twelve categories and an alert that you have overspent on “entertainment” — and you still do not know the one thing you wanted: how much you can spend today without it hurting.

Claroni answers that one question. Everything else comes after it.

What we believe

Less data, more clarity. Most finance apps compete on how much they can show you. We think clarity comes from taking away rather than adding — and that the work is done well when you no longer need to look at the screen.

Tracking your money should not be a chore either. We do not want you entering things, tagging them, or sitting down with a spreadsheet every week. You connect a card and it runs itself.

And one more thing: money is a sensitive subject. An app that tells you off for spending gets abandoned. Claroni tells you where you stand, not what you did wrong.

How we work

  • One paid plan, one price. No tiers, no add-ons, no negotiating.
  • No advertising. We do not sell your data to anyone and never will — in a finance app that would be especially shabby.
  • Read-only access. Claroni cannot move money out of your account, because it has no permission to.
  • Cancel in two taps. No phone call, no reason, no delaying tactics.

Who it is for

For students, early founders, and anyone whose income has no shape to it. If you have a steady salary and a mortgage, there are apps that will serve you better than we will — and it is fair to say so up front.
